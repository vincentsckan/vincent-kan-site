> 🇪🇸 March 25, 1971, outside a Spanish Navy radar base—soldiers opened fire in the dark on a humanoid figure over 2 meters tall, bullets passing through it like a ghost, leaving it unharmed. The next day, a section of barbed wire fence vanished without a trace. This is not a sci-fi movie, but an experience that then-Army Corporal **Jesus Jofre Mila** dared to fully recount only decades later.

## 🌙 An Uneasy Night: The Guard Dogs First Detected Something Wrong

On the night of March 25, 1971, everything at a Spanish Navy radar base seemed normal—until the guard dogs began acting strangely.

The base's German Shepherds—trained rigorously for night patrols—suddenly became extremely agitated and aggressive, completely out of control. Mila recalled that the dogs reacted as if sensing something "completely unfamiliar."

Soon after, a sentry pointed to the sky. Above the radar dome, a **disc-shaped craft** appeared silently. It was described as a classic **double-layered oval**, emitting a **golden glow**—bright enough to clearly illuminate the object's outline, yet oddly not blinding.

Multiple soldiers witnessed the object slowly cross the horizon, then **instantly accelerate** toward the sea, disappearing into the night.

## 💥 A Dazzling Flash Over the Sea

But the night was far from over.

Another soldier stationed near the rocky coastline later reported seeing the object dive rapidly toward the sea surface. When it touched the water, it emitted an **extremely intense flash**—so powerful that it **temporarily blinded him**.

When other soldiers found him later, he was still clutching his eyes. His eyes were red and swollen for days, and base medical personnel could not explain the injury. **No reports of conventional explosions, aircraft, or weapons fire** were made that night.

## 🟢 Green Light Beyond the Fence

Later that night, Mila and another soldier, accompanied by guard dogs, noticed an anomaly while patrolling along the fence—an **eerie green glow** on the open ground outside the base boundary.

Armed, they approached the light source with the dogs. As they advanced, Mila described the air feeling "charged," like static electricity. He even felt a **tingling numbness on his tongue**—like touching a battery. Even the guard dogs seemed distressed, shaking their heads.

Then they heard it.

Heavy footsteps—fast, purposeful, and **unnervingly close**.

## 👤 Face to Face: A Humanoid Over 2 Meters Tall

Out of the darkness emerged an extremely tall figure, **estimated to be about 2.1 to 2.2 meters (7 feet to 7 feet 2 inches) tall**.

The soldiers shouted commands, but there was no response.

In panic, one soldier fired first. Mila then raised his 9mm pistol and fired several shots. The muzzle flash briefly illuminated the figure.

What they saw—was not human.

The being was described as:

- **Extremely tall and thin**
- **Pale skin**
- **Long, light-colored hair reaching the shoulders**
- Wearing a **tight, metallic-looking suit** with a **high collar**
- A **inverted triangle symbol** at the center of its belt

The bullets seemed to have no effect.

Mila recalled a bizarre moment—he felt as if **time itself had stopped**. Then the humanoid **calmly turned and walked away**, disappearing toward the fence.

## 🔗 The Vanished Barbed Wire

Minutes later, the soldiers heard the sound of metal clashing.

At dawn, the evidence was undeniable: **a section of the inner barbed wire fence, about 50 cm wide, had completely vanished**. The edges were scorched, as if cut or disintegrated. Strangely, **the outer fence was intact**.

There were no footprints, no debris, no mechanical explanation. The missing section was later crudely patched with wire.

## 😴 Dreams, Voices, and the Sky's Return

In the following nights, Mila experienced intense dreams related to the encounter. One night, he reported hearing **overlapping voices in his head**—initially incomprehensible. Days later, he claimed the messages became clear, but he remains cautious about their content.

**On March 27—two days after the incident**—the group of lights reappeared. This time, it was **witnessed by dozens of personnel**, possibly lasting for hours. Mila recalled the lights performing complex maneuvers and formations above the base.

Base officers sternly warned everyone not to discuss the matter.

## 🇪🇸 Why This Case Matters

This is not an isolated incident. The 1970s saw multiple UFO-related military encounters reported across the Spanish peninsula:

- 1975: Spanish Air Force fighters chased a UFO over the **Canary Islands**
- 1976: A glowing orb appeared over **Tenerife**, prompting the military to fire hundreds of shells
- 1979: A UFO and helicopter had a close encounter near **Almería**

But among these, **the 1971 "shooting incident" is most unique**—because it involved soldiers directly confronting a physical humanoid presence, leaving verifiable physical evidence (the vanished fence), and the witness remained cautious years later.
