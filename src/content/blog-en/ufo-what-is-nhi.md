## What is NHI?

**NHI = Non-Human Intelligence**

This is the latest official term used by the U.S. government and military to describe the possible intelligence behind UAP. This term frequently appears in documents from AARO (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office).

## Why use NHI instead of aliens?

The scope of NHI is broader than "aliens," including:

| Possibility | Description |
|--------|---'
---|
| 👽 Extraterrestrial Intelligence | Civilizations from other planets |
| 🌍 Earth-based Native Intelligence | Unknown intelligent beings hidden on Earth |
| 🌀 Cross-dimensional Intelligence | Entities existing in other dimensions |
| 🤖 Advanced AI | Non-biological intelligence |
| ⏳ Time Travelers | Future humans returning to monitor history |

## David Grusch's NHI Testimony

In 2023, whistleblower David Grusch testified before Congress, claiming that the U.S. government possesses biological remains and vehicles of "Non-Human Intelligence" (NHI). His testimony brought the term NHI into the global media spotlight.

Grusch stated: "I am absolutely certain, based on conversations with over 40 current or retired intelligence officials, that the U.S. government has had NHI biological remains since the 1930s."

## What does the scientific community think about NHI?

The real scientific questions are not "whether NHI exists," but:
1. If it exists, how to communicate with them?
2. How does their technology work?
3. What impact do they have on human society?
