> 📍 **Canada, Nova Scotia, Shag Harbour**
> 📅 **October 4, 1967**
> 🔍 **Water crash, naval search, government archives**

On the evening of October 4, 1967, residents of Shag Harbour, Nova Scotia, Canada, saw a large, humming, luminous object crash into the Atlantic Ocean. Over **11 people** independently reported witnessing this event.

The Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) and the Canadian Coast Guard immediately launched a search. Navy divers went underwater to investigate and discovered strange marks and foam on the seabed.

![Shag Harbour](/vincent-kan-site/_astro/blog-placeholder-2.Bx0Zcyzv.jpg)
*Shag Harbour, Nova Scotia, Canada — UFO crash site*

Records from the Canadian Department of National Defence, the RCMP, and the Department of Transport all mention a "UFO" crash. The Canadian government later declassified the relevant files, making the Shag Harbour incident one of the few UFO cases acknowledged by **official government records**.
