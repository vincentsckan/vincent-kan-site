> 📍 **Gobi Desert, Mongolia**
> 📅 **1980s**
> 🔍 **Spy rumors, Soviet archives**

During the Cold War, there was a persistent claim within Western intelligence circles: the Soviet Union had established a secret UFO research base deep in the Gobi Desert of Mongolia. It was alleged that the Soviets recovered technology from a crashed UFO and conducted reverse engineering at this isolated location.

U.S. spy satellites reportedly captured images of unusually large facilities in the area, but the information was never officially confirmed. After the collapse of the Soviet Union, some Russian officers vaguely alluded to similar projects in interviews, yet direct evidence has always been lacking. To this day, this rumor remains one of the most mysterious Cold War stories in UFO research.
