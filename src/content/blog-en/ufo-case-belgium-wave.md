## Event Overview

![Belgian F-16 Fighter Jet](/vincent-kan-site/_astro/blog-placeholder-2.Bx0Zcyzv.jpg)
*Belgian Air Force F-16 fighter jet once locked onto a triangular UFO*

Between November 1989 and April 1990, large-scale unidentified flying object reports occurred over Belgium.

## Key Event: F-16 Interception

In the early hours of March 30-31, 1990, the Belgian Air Force ordered two F-16 fighter jets to intercept. The jets' radar successfully locked onto the target, recording:
- The object accelerated from a standstill to 1,770 km/h in a short time
- Dropped from 3,000 feet to 1,000 feet instantly
- Radar lock lasted approximately 1 hour
- The object performed maneuvers impossible for human aircraft

## Sighting Characteristics

- **Shape**: Large triangle, with bright lights at each corner
- **Color**: Usually dark black, with white/red/green lights on the bottom
- **Sound**: Completely silent
- **Flight Mode**: Capable of stationary hovering and instant acceleration

## Scientific Investigation

The Belgian Air Force and the National Fund for Scientific Research (FNRS) formed the "Belgian UFO Investigation Group" (SOBEPS), one of the few government-led scientific investigations into UFOs.

## Historical Significance

The Belgian UFO wave is one of the few cases with military radar records, fighter jet lock-ons, and a large number of witnesses. It is regarded by the research community as one of the most compelling pieces of evidence in the UFO phenomenon.
