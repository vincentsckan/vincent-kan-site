> 📍 **Pacific Ocean, USS Omaha (US Navy)**
> 📅 **2019**
> 🔍 **Officially Confirmed, Transmedium UAP**

In 2019, crew members of the US Navy littoral combat ship **USS Omaha (LCS-12)** captured a spherical unidentified flying object using a night vision system. The footage shows a glowing orb descending slowly from the air to the sea surface, then submerging into the water without creating any splash.

## Official Confirmation

In 2021, the Pentagon officially confirmed that this footage was recorded by Navy personnel and classified it as a UAP incident. Unlike the previous Nimitz incident, the USS Omaha footage is **the first transmedium UAP case recorded simultaneously by shipboard radar and optical systems**.

## Transmedium Capability

This incident solidified a key finding in UAP research: some UAPs possess **"transmedium"** capability—the ability to transition directly from air into water without any deceleration or resistance. Known human technology cannot currently achieve this.
