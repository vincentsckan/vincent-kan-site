## Brazilian Air Force Radar Anomalies

The Brazilian Air Force released on January 5 anomalous logs from the Tabatinga radar station in Amazonas state for December 2025. The data shows 7 unidentified objects performing irregular maneuvers at speeds over 2,000 km/h at altitudes of 3,000-8,000 meters between December 8-15, with no transponder signals. Brazil's Aeronautical Accident Investigation and Prevention Center said the records have been shared with US AARO for joint analysis.
