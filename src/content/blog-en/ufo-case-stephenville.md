> 📍 **Stephenville, Texas, USA**
> 📅 **January 8, 2008**
> 🔍 **Mass Sighting, Multiple Videos**

On the evening of January 8, 2008, a series of UFO sighting reports emerged in the town of Stephenville, Texas. Residents described a massive object flying low over the town, estimated to be about 1 mile in length. The object emitted bright lights and caused the ground to shake.

Over 200 people reported sightings, including a county sheriff, businessmen, and aviation professionals. The diverse backgrounds of the witnesses made the case difficult to dismiss. Multiple amateur videos and subsequent MUFON investigation reports further confirmed the authenticity of the event. The military initially denied any activity, but later acknowledged that F-16 fighter jets were training in the area, yet could not explain the massive object described by witnesses.
