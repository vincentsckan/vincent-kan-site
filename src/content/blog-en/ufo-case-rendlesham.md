## Incident Overview

![Rendlesham Forest](/vincent-kan-site/_astro/blog-placeholder-3.Bx0Zcyzv.jpg)
*Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, England – location of the UFO incident*

In the early hours of December 26, 1980, U.S. Air Force personnel stationed at RAF Woodbridge reported mysterious lights in the nearby Rendlesham Forest.

This incident is known as **"Britain's Roswell"** and is one of the few UAP cases involving direct contact by military personnel, physical ground traces, and radar confirmation.

## Key Figures

### Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt
Deputy base commander, who led the investigation on the second night of the incident:
- He personally observed red, blue, and white flashing lights in the forest
- Recorded the entire investigation process using a tape recorder
- Later wrote the famous "Halt Memo" submitted to the Ministry of Defence

### Sergeant Jim Penniston
The first military person to arrive at the scene:
- Witnessed a solid, triangular object with mysterious symbols on its surface
- Was able to touch the object, which felt smooth and warm
- The object later rose vertically from the ground and disappeared

## Evidence

- **Ground Traces**: Three triangular indentations at the location where the object rested
- **Radiation Readings**: Geiger counters showed abnormal radiation levels
- **Radar Records**: Base radar confirmed an unidentified object
- **Halt Recording**: Military audio recording documented the entire investigation
- **Tree Damage**: Burn marks on branches above the object's location

## UK Ministry of Defence Response

The UK Ministry of Defence initially denied the incident but later acknowledged that investigation records did exist. Documents show the MOD classified the incident at a level indicating a "possible threat to national security."

## Case Impact

Rendlesham Forest has become the most famous UAP research site in the UK, attracting numerous researchers and tourists each year. The wealth of evidence makes it one of the most compelling UAP cases.
