## Limitations of Human Technology

Currently, the fastest human-made aircraft (X-43A) reaches a speed of approximately 11,200 km/h, but the performance of the Tic Tac UAP during the Nimitz incident far exceeds this figure.

## Possible Propulsion Theories

### 1. Inertia Cancellation
- Principle: Cancels the inertial mass of an object
- Effect: Extreme acceleration without generating G-forces
- Status: Theoretically feasible, but requires immense energy

### 2. Gravity Field Manipulation
- Principle: Locally warping spacetime
- Effect: Moves "space itself" rather than using thrust
- Status: Allowed by Einstein's General Relativity, but practically unattainable

### 3. Zero-Point Energy Extraction
- Principle: Extracting energy from the quantum vacuum
- Effect: Infinite energy, no fuel required
- Status: Controversial, but studied by NASA's Eagleworks laboratory

### 4. Plasma Stealth
- Principle: Enveloping the aircraft in plasma
- Effect: Absorbs radar waves, creates its own optical illusions
- Status: Preliminary research conducted by Russia and China

## Military Speculation

Former AARO Director **Sean Kirkpatrick** has suggested that some UAP technology surpasses that of the United States by at least **50-100 years**.
