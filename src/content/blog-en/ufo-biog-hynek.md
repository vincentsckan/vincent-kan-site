> 📍 **United States** | 📅 **1910-1986** | 🔍 **Biography, Scientific Research**

**Josef Allen Hynek** (Dr. Hynek) is one of the most important figures in the history of UFO research. Originally an astronomer, he was hired by the U.S. Air Force to serve as a scientific consultant for Project Blue Book.

Initially, Hynek was a thorough skeptic, believing that 99% of UFO reports could be explained by conventional phenomena. But as his research deepened, he discovered a small fraction of cases that were simply inexplicable.

## Close Encounter Classification System

Hynek created the classification system still in use today:

| Classification | Description |
|------|------|
| **First Kind** | Night lights / Daylight discs |
| **Second Kind** | Radar + visual confirmation simultaneously |
| **Third Kind** | Sighting of alien beings |
| **Fourth Kind** | Abduction by alien beings |
| **Fifth Kind** | Direct communication with alien beings |

## Scientific Contributions

In 1972, Hynek published *The UFO Experience: A Scientific Inquiry*, the first book to analyze UFOs using scientific methods. He also famously said:

> "UFO research is not pseudoscience. But if a scientist publicly studies UFOs, his career is over."
