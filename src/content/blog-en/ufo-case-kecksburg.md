> 📍 **United States, Kecksburg, Pennsylvania**
> 📅 **December 9, 1965**
> 🔍 **Crash event, military recovery**

On the afternoon of December 9, 1965, residents of Kecksburg, Pennsylvania heard a loud bang, then saw a smoke trail streaking across the sky. A giant acorn-shaped metal object crashed into the forest near Kecksburg.

![Kecksburg UFO crash illustration](/vincent-kan-site/_astro/blog-placeholder-2.Bx0Zcyzv.jpg)
*NASA footage of the acorn-shaped metal object residents claimed to have seen*

Military personnel quickly arrived and cordoned off the scene. Residents reported seeing mysterious hieroglyphic-like markings on the object. A flatbed truck removed the object that same night. The military's explanation was a "meteor" or "debris from a Soviet satellite," but residents insisted it was a man-made metallic object.
