## Overview

| 📍 *Hangzhou, China* | 📅 *July 7, 2010* | 🔍 *Airport Closure / Multiple Witnesses* |

On the night of July 7, 2010, pilots at Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport (HGH) reported an unidentified flying object appearing over the runway. Airport authorities immediately initiated emergency procedures, suspending all flights for an hour.
