## 🇺🇸 Trump: "UFO Files Are Very Interesting, Will Be Released Soon"

**Source:** AP News / NBC News (May 3, 2026)

Trump has once again made his latest remarks on UFO disclosure, stating that he has found a large number of "very interesting documents" among the classified government files he is reviewing. Trump had already announced on Truth Social in February that he would instruct the Secretary of War and other relevant departments to begin "identifying and releasing government files related to extraterrestrial life, UAPs, and UFOs." 3️⃣ **Global Conflict Threat** — With the risk of nuclear war escalating, Bassett believes extraterrestrial civilizations have been monitoring humanity's nuclear weapons development

Bassett further noted: "This is no coincidence — since the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, contact with these non-human entities and their technology has increased dramatically. They have hovered over US and Russian bases and shut down ICBMs."

## 👩‍🔬 UFO Scientist Amy Eskridge: Stalked, Threatened, and Subjected to Kidnapping Attempt Before Death

**Source:** Daily Mail (May 3, 2026)

A 34-year-old female scientist **Amy Eskridge**, who researched anti-gravity propulsion technology, was found dead from a gunshot wound to the head in June 2022, officially ruled a suicide. But recently leaked recordings and conversations have reignited controversy over the case.

Phone recordings provided by former British intelligence officer **Franc Milburn** reveal that Eskridge was monitored and threatened for years before her death:

- 🗣️ **Airport Kidnapping Attempt** — In the recordings, Eskridge described how, when flying from Virginia back to Alabama, military and airport personnel seemed to have been tipped off in advance, rushing her through security. After landing, she found her car vandalized, the battery removed, and pepper spray emptied. A "colonel" then suddenly appeared, claiming to help her "cross the river"
- 🏠 **Home Invasions** — Her residence was broken into multiple times, with large shoe prints left in the backyard, attempting to intimidate her into stopping her research
- ⚡ **Directed Energy Weapon Attack** — Eskridge claimed before her death that she was attacked with a "directed energy weapon," causing burns and discoloration on her hands

Eskridge's father, a former NASA scientist, has consistently rejected the "suicide" ruling. FBI Director **Kash Patel** previously stated that he is "leading the investigation" into multiple cases of missing and deceased scientists.

## 🔍 The Bizarre Deaths of UFO Researchers: A Decades-Long Pattern

**Source:** Daily Mail (May 3, 2026)

The Daily Mail has compiled a record of bizarre deaths among UFO researchers, revealing a chilling pattern spanning decades.

Since 2022, at least **11 prominent scientists, nuclear officials, and UFO experts** have died or disappeared under mysterious circumstances, including retired Major General **William Neil McCasland**. The FBI is investigating whether these cases are connected.

But researcher **Timothy Hood** points out that this pattern can actually be traced back to the late 1940s — the dawn of the flying saucer era:

- 👻 **1947 Maury Island Incident** — Harold Dahl reported seeing six golden and silver donut-shaped objects, after which he was threatened by men in black to keep quiet. Air Force officers Davidson and Brown, who went to investigate, died in a B-25 crash on their return flight
- 🔫 **Journalist Paul Lance** — Died suddenly of meningitis two weeks after reporting on related events
- 🛩️ **Pilot Kenneth Arnold** — Experienced engine failure on takeoff, finding the fuel valve had been turned off
- 🕵️ **UFO Researcher Philip Schneider** (1996) — Found dead at home with a rubber tube around his neck, officially ruled a suicide, but his ex-wife and friends reject this

Author **Nigel Watson** stated: "Many of these cases may be coincidences, but there are indeed some very strange events."
