## 🇧🇷 Brazil, April 2026: An "Orb Wave" of Spherical UAPs

In late April 2026, multiple states in southeastern Brazil (mainly **São Paulo state** and **Minas Gerais state**) experienced a large-scale **spherical UAP sighting wave**. Numerous cellphone videos circulated widely online, becoming a focus of the global UAP community.

### Key Event Details

- **Date**: April 27, 2026, afternoon (UTC-3 time zone, approximately 14:00-16:00)
- **Location**: Multiple towns including Campinas (SP), Belo Horizonte (MG), Uberlândia, and Ribeirão Preto
- **Number of Sightings**: Witnesses reported seeing 5 to 12 luminous orbs, orange-white in color
- **Characteristics**: Orbs approximately 1-3 meters in diameter, flying silently at altitudes of 500-2,000 feet, performing physically impossible maneuvers such as instant acceleration, 90-degree sharp turns, and deceleration without slowing down

### Evidence and Analysis

A 48-second video from Campinas shows about 8 orange-white orbs hovering over a highway, filmed by a driver named João Silva. It garnered 1.2 million views on X (Twitter) within 24 hours. Another 1-minute 22-second multi-angle video from Belo Horizonte, filmed by three witnesses (including a local journalist), shows the orbs splitting and recombining.

Industry analysis indicates that **conventional explanations (birds, balloons, drones) are difficult to sustain**:

| Explanation | Why It Doesn't Hold Up |
|------|----------------|
| Drone Swarms | No radio signals detected; speed and altitude exceed Brazilian civil aviation regulations; completely silent |
| Birds/Sky Lanterns | Daytime conditions rule out sky lanterns; movements exceed bird capabilities |
| Military Tests | The Brazilian Air Force (FAB) has denied conducting related activities and issued no NOTAMs |

### Official Response

The Brazilian Air Force's UFO investigation committee (which has released public archives since 2010) stated it is investigating. A preliminary statement noted: "Civilian and military sensors have confirmed anomalous aerial contacts that pose no threat." As of April 29, the orbs were reported to have flown eastward toward the Atlantic Ocean.

## 📚 New Academic Discovery: Mysterious Flashes on Cold War Astronomical Plates

At the same time, a peer-reviewed paper published in October 2025 in **Scientific Reports** presents a startling finding: astronomical observation plates from the 1950s Cold War era recorded a large number of **brief mysterious flashes (transients)**, which show a statistical correlation with **ground nuclear tests** and **UFO reports**.

### Research Details

A team led by **Dr. Beatriz Villarroel** (Nordic Institute for Theoretical Physics, Nordita) and **Dr. Stephen Bruehl** (Vanderbilt University Medical Center) analyzed over 100,000 transient flash phenomena captured by the **Palomar Observatory Sky Survey (POSS-I)** between 1949 and 1957.

Key Findings:

1. **Nuclear Test Correlation**: The probability of these flashes appearing within one day of a nuclear test increased by **45%**; it peaked at **68%** one day after a test
2. **UFO Report Correlation**: For each additional UFO report in the UFOCAT database, the number of flashes on the same day increased by an average of **8.5%**
3. **Non-Traditional Explanations**: These flashes do not resemble defects caused by plate dust or radioactive contamination, and their temporal pattern (especially the peak one day after nuclear tests) does not match the physical mechanisms of general explosion debris

### Two Possibilities

The research team proposes two directions:

- **Atmospheric Phenomena**: Nuclear explosions may trigger some unrecognized atmospheric phenomena, producing brief point-like flashes
- **Orbital Objects**: A more speculative explanation—some flashes may be high-altitude/orbital objects that existed before artificial satellites, potentially related to UFO sightings

The team emphasized: "Our findings provide additional empirical support for the reality of the UAP phenomenon and its potential connection to nuclear weapons activities, supplementing data beyond mere witness reports."

This research is part of the **VASCO (Vanishing and Appearing Sources during a Century of Observations)** project, and independent corroboration has already been provided by replication studies using other astronomical plate collections.
