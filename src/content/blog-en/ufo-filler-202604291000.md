Back in January 1996, a series of bizarre events unfolded in the small southeastern Brazilian city of Varginha, which to this day remains one of the most significant cases in global UFO research. Unlike the ancient Roswell case, Varginha has military records from the time, hospital reports, and a large number of witnesses, along with descriptions suspected to be of "extraterrestrial beings."

In January 2026, on the 30th anniversary of this case, a group of Brazilian witnesses flew to Washington, D.C., along with American filmmaker James Fox, for a closed-door meeting with three members of Congress on Capitol Hill, followed by a press conference at the National Press Club to unveil new evidence. **This story is also reported firsthand by Leslie Kean and Ralph Blumenthal (the two journalists who broke the NYT story on the "Navy UFO videos"), giving it substantial weight.**

## What Happened That Day?

| 📍 *Varginha, Brazil* | 📅 *January 1996* | 🔍 *Historical UFO Sighting/Alien Encounter* |
| --- | --- | --- |

- **January 13:** Geography teacher and ultralight aircraft pilot Carlos de Sousa spotted a cigar-shaped craft trailing smoke and crashing in the countryside. He got out of his car to look, smelled a strong ammonia odor, and found metal fragments that could be crumpled like tin foil but would spring back. Grass within 40 meters was scorched. Soon after, the military arrived and pointed guns at him, ordering him to leave.
- **January 20:** Three teenage girls (ages 14-22) were walking home from school when they saw a creature about 4 feet tall squatting in a vacant lot. They described it as having brown, oily skin, a large head with three protrusions, red eyes the size of balls, vein-like spots on its body, and emitting a foul odor. Witnesses said its eyes looked sad and terrified, as if it were asking for help.
- **Subsequent days:** A large number of military vehicles and ambulances appeared on the streets of Varginha. Rumors circulated that the military had captured at least two creatures, one of which was taken to a local hospital.
- Police officer Marco Eli Chereze is said to have had direct physical contact with a creature and died of an unknown infection on February 7, just days later.
- It is suspected that the military transported the bodies via Campinas University, after which they were handed over to the U.S. side and shipped out of the country.

## The Big Washington D.C. Reveal 30 Years Later

The January 2026 event was divided into two parts:

### 1️⃣ Closed-Door Meeting on Capitol Hill (January 15)
Three members of Congress (including prominent UAP advocate Tim Burchett) met with the Brazilian team in their offices. The team included:
- Neurosurgeon Dr. Italo Venturelli
- Forensic pathologist Dr. Armando Fortunato
- Original witness Carlos de Sousa

Among them, the neurosurgeon mentioned a chilling detail: **He looked directly into the pale purple eyes of a "non-human intelligence" and sensed an extremely high level of intelligence.**

### 2️⃣ National Press Club Press Conference (January 20)
- James Fox presented six new video testimonies from witnesses
- Former high-ranking Brazilian officials broke their confidentiality agreements to testify
- The autopsy report of military police officer Marco Chereze was disclosed — suggesting his death may be linked to contact with a non-human entity
- Fox said he now knows who holds video footage of the alien creature, adding, "I never give up"
- Ross Coulthart reported live from the scene, describing the evidence as "very compelling"

## What Does the Official Side Say? — Brazilian Military Files Declassified

Interestingly, on January 6, 2026, the Brazilian Superior Tribunal Militar (STM) released all the military investigation files from that time. The official conclusion was simple: the whole thing was a "product of misunderstandings and rumors." They claimed the girls had actually seen a mentally disabled homeless man named "Mudinho," who often squatted on the street, and that poor weather and dim lighting caused him to be mistaken for an alien.

**But...** there are major contradictions between the military files and witness testimonies:
- Why did the girls' description differ so drastically from that of an ordinary homeless person? (Brown, oily skin? Big eyes with three protrusions?)
- Why did the military mobilize so heavily on the same day?
- Why did prosecutor Chereze suddenly die from an unknown infection?
- Why have so many soldiers and firefighters come forward to speak decades later?

## Key Points to Note

James Fox met with AARO (the U.S. Department of Defense's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) in 2024 to discuss the Varginha case. Fox said he asked AARO Director Dr. Jon T. Kosloski whether there were any plans to disclose the truth to the public. Kosloski's response was classic:

> **"I can't even comb my hair without Pentagon approval. You can quote me on that."**

That statement almost sums up the entire dilemma of the UFO issue.

## Summary

For thirty years, the Varginha mystery has lacked solid physical evidence — no publicly available photos, videos, or official documents can confirm the whole story. However, over twenty independent witnesses, each experiencing different parts of the event, have pieced together a coherent narrative — from the crash to the capture of the creature, from the military lockdown to the covert U.S. involvement.

The fact that heavyweight journalists Leslie Kean and Ralph Blumenthal are willing to follow up on this story itself tells everyone that this is no ordinary ufology chatter. With more witnesses coming forward and the gradual declassification of Brazilian military files, Varginha may be the international UFO case closest to being solved since Roswell.
