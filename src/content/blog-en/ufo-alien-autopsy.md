> 📍 **UK/Global** | 📅 **1995** | 🔍 **Controversial Media, Hoax Event**

## Event Background

In 1995, British producer **Ray Santilli** released a black-and-white film claiming to have been shot at the 1947 Roswell crash site. The footage showed military personnel performing an autopsy on a "grey alien" corpse.

## Global Sensation

- Fox Television purchased the rights for global broadcast
- Ratings skyrocketed, with over 15 million US households watching
- Sparked renewed global interest in the Roswell incident

## Controversial Truth

Years later, Santilli admitted the film was not authentic, but rather a "reconstruction" based on original footage (which he claimed had deteriorated). However, he has always maintained that the original 1947 footage did exist.

## Cultural Impact

Even as a hoax, this film had a massive influence:
- Fueled the *X-Files* craze
- Brought UFO culture into the mainstream
- Boosted Roswell tourism dramatically
- Also made people much more skeptical of "UFO footage" from then on
