### 🔥 Schumer-Rounds Introduce Standalone UAP Disclosure Bill — Bypassing NDAA for Direct Legislation

**Sources:** Congress.gov, Politico, The Debrief (April 21–28, 2026)

Senate Majority Leader **Chuck Schumer (D-NY)** and Senator **Mike Rounds (R-SD)** formally introduced the **UAP Disclosure Act of 2026 (S. 4521)** on April 21 as a standalone bill, bypassing the often-stalled National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) framework.

**📋 Core Bill Provisions (36 pages total):**

| Provision | Details |
|------|----'
---|
| **Mandatory Declassification** | Requires all federal agencies (DoD, CIA, FBI, etc.) to review and declassify UAP records within 270 days |
| **Independent Review Board** | Establishes a 9-member independent commission appointed by the President and congressional leaders, with the authority to overturn classification decisions |
| **Covers Private Sector** | Mandates disclosure of UAP materials held by contractors (e.g., Lockheed Martin), explicitly covering evidence of "non-human intelligence" |
| **Penalties** | Non-compliance results in fines/imprisonment; prohibits destruction of records |
| **Timeline** | Archive to open by January 1, 2027; full public disclosure targeted by December 31, 2028 |

**🗣️ Schumer on the Senate Floor (April 21):**
> *"The American people deserve the truth... we've waited too long for half-measures."*

**🗣️ Rounds Emphasizes National Security:**
Noted that AARO has received over 800 UAP reports since 2021, 21 of which exhibit "anomalous characteristics."

**⚠️ Significant Opposition:**
- During an April 25 Senate debate, Minority Leader **Mitch McConnell (R-KY)** used procedural maneuvers to block Sections 1842 (public disclosure timeline) and 1843 (civilian oversight), citing "excessive executive power" and "harm to intelligence sources"
- The revised NDAA version has removed core disclosure provisions, retaining only vague reporting requirements
- Schumer has gathered **45 co-sponsors**, vowing to continue pushing the standalone bill
- On the House side, Rep. Burchett and Rep. Luna have introduced a companion version, H.R. 1187

**🔮 Outlook:** The bill needs 60 votes to pass the Senate. Schumer hopes to attach it to an upcoming must-pass bill. On April 28, the Senate sent the NDAA to a conference committee with a 99-1 vote; whether UAP provisions will be reinstated remains uncertain.

### 🚀 Cold War Nuclear Missile Base UAP Cases Re-ignite Discussion — Former NASA Scientist Analyzes

**Sources:** Mayim Bialik's Breakdown Podcast, International Business Times (April 28, 2026)

Former NASA scientist **Dr. Kevin Knuth** discussed in detail on the *Mayim Bialik's Breakdown* podcast historical incidents from the Cold War where UAPs appeared near U.S. nuclear missile bases, causing **anomalous shutdowns of missile systems**, re-igniting public interest in the connection between nuclear facilities and UAPs.

**🔍 Key Case: 1967 Malmstrom Air Force Base, Montana Incident**
- Multiple former Air Force personnel reported that after a luminous object appeared over the base, several **Minuteman intercontinental ballistic missiles** simultaneously became inoperable
- The missile guidance system failures coincided **exactly** with the UAP sighting
- This is the most famous of several similar incidents during the Cold War

**🗣️ Knuth's Position:**
> *"These are only anecdotal evidence, unverified, possibly stemming from technical malfunctions or electromagnetic interference... but the pattern is too consistent to ignore and warrants further investigation."*

**⚠️ Official Stance:**
- The U.S. Department of Defense and AARO state such claims lack verification
- Many cases are attributed to **inherent flaws in 1960s missile technology** or sensor anomalies
- Knuth himself emphasizes prioritizing conventional explanations, noting that while the extreme speeds and accelerations of UAPs challenge known technology, they don't necessarily represent extraterrestrial involvement
