> 📍 **United States, Texas**
> 📅 **December 29, 1980**
> 🔍 **Radiation injuries, legal proceedings**

On the evening of December 29, 1980, Betty Cash, Vickie Landrum, and her grandson Colby Landrum were driving through a forest in Texas when they saw a huge, diamond-shaped luminous object hovering above the road. The object emitted extremely intense heat, causing a burning sensation to those inside the car.

## Aftermath

- The three suffered severe nausea and vomiting
- Skin showed burn marks (resembling sunburn but more severe)
- Significant hair loss
- Long-term health issues (chronic fatigue, weakened immune system)

## Military Helicopters

Witnesses also reported that multiple CH-47 Chinook heavy-lift helicopters were surrounding the UFO. They suspected the military was recovering or testing some kind of secret aircraft.

## Legal Proceedings

Betty Cash and the Landrum family later sued the U.S. government, seeking $217,000 in compensation. However, the court ultimately dismissed the lawsuit, citing "national security."
