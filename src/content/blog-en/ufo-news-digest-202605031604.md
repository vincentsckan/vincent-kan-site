### 1️⃣ 🇺🇸 Trump Drops Hint: 'Very Interesting' UFO Files to Be Released Soon

On April 29, during a White House event with NASA astronauts, Trump hinted that the Pentagon is preparing to release a batch of "never-before-seen" UFO-related documents. He said: "We're going to release a lot of things that have never been released before, and I think some of them will be very interesting to everyone."

In February, Trump had already ordered federal agencies to fully declassify records related to UFOs and extraterrestrial life, but this is the first time he has explicitly stated that "unreleased documents" are about to come out. The White House and the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) are collaborating to prepare "unprecedented UAP information."

However, former AARO director Sean Kirkpatrick (a physicist) poured cold water on the idea, saying these files won't contain any earth-shattering secrets. "Readers shouldn't expect photos or records of alien visits to Earth, because those things simply don't exist."

### 2️⃣ 💔 Renowned UFO Researcher David Wilcock Dies at 53

The UFO research community has received tragic news — renowned UFO researcher and regular on the documentary series *Ancient Aliens*, David Wilcock, has died by suicide in Boulder County, Colorado. The coroner's office confirmed that he took his own life with a firearm at 11:02 a.m. on April 20.

Two days before the incident, Wilcock posted on X (Twitter): "I'm not sure if I'll do the show tomorrow... Some really intense things are happening lately." His YouTube channel, "Divine Cosmos with David Wilcock," had accumulated over 46 million views and 518,000 subscribers.

Congresswoman Anna Paulina Luna posted a tribute on X, calling his death a "tragedy" and quoting a Bible verse: "You will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."

### 4️⃣ 🔍 FBI and Congress Investigate Mysterious Scientist Deaths/Disappearances

The House of Representatives and the FBI are jointly investigating a series of deaths and disappearances of scientists, involving about 10 top researchers who had participated in U.S. nuclear energy and space research. Their work covered areas including nuclear fusion, advanced metallurgy, and even UFO research.

Committee Chairman James Comer said: "This is very concerning and a national security issue. There may be some shady things going on."

FBI Director Kash Patel stated: "If any conduct leads to wrongdoing or conspiracy, we will make appropriate arrests."

Cases under investigation include:
- **William Neil McCasland** — Retired Air Force general, disappeared in New Mexico
- **Monica Reza** — Former NASA scientist, disappeared while hiking in Angeles National Forest
- **Nuno F.G. Loureiro** — Renowned MIT physics professor, shot dead at his home in Boston in December
- **Amy Catherine Eskridge** — Anti-gravity researcher, died at age 34 in 2022 (ruled a suicide)

When asked about this, Trump responded that it was "quite serious," hoping it was just a coincidence, but that the government would follow up with an investigation.
