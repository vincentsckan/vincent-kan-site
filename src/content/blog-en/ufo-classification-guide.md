## Hynek Classic Classification (1972)

The most classic system created by J. Allen Hynek:

**Close Encounters**
| Level | Definition | Example |
|-------|------------|------'
---|
| CE-1 | Sight UFO but no impact | Most sightings |
| CE-2 | UFO leaves physical traces | Cash-Landrum |
| CE-3 | Sight alien beings | Ariel School |
| CE-4 | Abduction | Travis Walton |
| CE-5 | Active communication | Steven Greer |

## Vallee Classification (1969)

Jacques Vallee's more detailed system divides into 8 MA (Manifestation) types, emphasizing the physical and psychological impact of UFOs.

## Modern UAP Classification (AARO 2022)

Modern classification used by the U.S. Department of Defense AARO:

- **Airborne UAP**: Unidentified objects within the atmosphere
- **Waterborne UAP**: Unidentified objects in oceans/lakes (trans-medium)
- **Spaceborne UAP**: Unidentified objects in Earth orbit

## NUFORC Classification

Classification from NUFORC, the world's largest sighting report database:
- **Triangle** (most common)
- **Sphere/Orb** (most common)
- **Disc** (classic)
- **Cigar** (large size)
- **Diamond**
- **Irregular shape**
