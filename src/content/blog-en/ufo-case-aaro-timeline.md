In 2007, then-Senator Harry Reid spearheaded the launch of a classified program—the **Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)**—managed by the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA). The project was executed by the company of billionaire and Reid's friend Robert Bigelow, with an annual budget of $22 million.

Program director Luis Elizondo later became a key figure in publicly advancing UAP disclosure.

📺 Above is the Pentagon's officially released GIMBAL UAP video, captured by the FLIR system of an F/A-18 fighter jet.

## Watershed Moment: 2017

In December 2017, *The New York Times* published a front-page report revealing the existence of AATIP, while also releasing three UAP videos filmed by the U.S. Navy:
- **FLIR1** (Tic Tac incident)
- **GIMBAL** (gyroscopic video)
- **GOFAST** (high-speed flight video)

This report fundamentally changed public perception of the UFO topic.

## The UAPTF Period

In 2020, the Department of Defense established the **Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force (UAPTF)**, formally taking over the investigation. In June 2021, the UAPTF released its first official report, acknowledging that only 1 out of 144 UAP incidents could be explained.

## The AARO Era

In July 2022, the Department of Defense announced the creation of the **All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)**, led by Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick. AARO's mission includes:
- Standardizing UAP reporting mechanisms
- Conducting scientific investigations
- Coordinating cross-agency information sharing
- Publishing historical review reports

## Major Developments in 2023-2024

- **July 2023**: Whistleblower David Grusch testified before Congress, claiming the government possesses vehicles of "non-human intelligence"
- **2024**: Multiple congressional UAP hearings advanced legislation
- Bipartisan demands for transparency continue to intensify

## Future Outlook

UAP disclosure has transitioned from a fringe topic to a mainstream political issue. As legislative and investigative mechanisms continue to improve, more information is expected to be released in the future.
