> 📅 **2022 Year in Review** | 🔍 **Annual Summary**

## 2022 UAP Milestones

### January
- U.S. intelligence agencies mandated by law to release more UAP data
- Japan Air Self-Defense Force releases UFO handling guidelines

### May
- U.S. House of Representatives holds UAP hearing
- Deputy Secretary of Defense Ronald Moultrie testifies
- Confirms authenticity of UAP footage captured by Navy personnel

### July
- **AARO (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office)** officially established
- Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick appointed as director

### September-October
- NASA announces formation of independent UAP study group
- UK Ministry of Defence releases final batch of UFO files

### December
- UAP disclosure provisions included in the *2023 National Defense Authorization Act*
- Whistleblower protection mechanisms begin to be established
