> 📍 **Melbourne, Australia – Westall Primary School**
> 📅 **April 6, 1966**
> 🔍 **Mass Sighting, School Gag Order**

On the afternoon of April 6, 1966, students and teachers at Westall Primary School in Melbourne, Australia, saw a silver disc-shaped object hovering over the school playground. The object then landed in a nearby grassy field, rose vertically a few seconds later, and disappeared into the clouds at extreme speed.

![Westall School UFO Mass Sighting](/vincent-kan-site/_astro/blog-placeholder-1.Bx0Zcyzv.jpg)
*Westall Primary School – the site where 200 students and teachers witnessed a UFO*

Over **200 students and teachers** witnessed the event. Military personnel and mysterious men in black suits quickly arrived at the school, demanding that staff and students remain silent. The incident was deliberately covered up for decades.
