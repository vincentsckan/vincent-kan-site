> 📍 **Zimbabwe, Ariel School**
> 📅 **September 16, 1994**
> 🔍 **Third Kind Contact, Child Sighting, Academic Investigation**

On September 16, 1994, an event that shocked the world occurred at **Ariel School**, near Harare, the capital of Zimbabwe. During recess, 62 schoolchildren saw multiple silver glowing objects land in the bush behind the school, and "aliens" emerged.

![Ariel School](../../assets/blog-placeholder-3.jpg)
*Zimbabwe's Ariel School — 62 schoolchildren collectively witnessed extraterrestrial beings*

The witnesses described the extraterrestrial beings with remarkable consistency:
- About 1 meter tall
- Large black eyes
- Wearing black tight-fitting suits
- Communicating telepathic messages ("The Earth is being destroyed")

Harvard professor **John Mack** personally flew to Zimbabwe to investigate and interviewed most of the schoolchildren. His conclusion was: "The experiences described by these children are extremely consistent; I cannot explain them with conventional reasoning."

In 2019, the Netflix documentary *Ariel Phenomenon* revisited the witnesses from that time — now grown adults — who still insist their experience that day was real.
