## Who is Grusch?

**David Charles Grusch** is a retired U.S. Air Force officer and former senior intelligence official at the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA) and the National Reconnaissance Office (NRO). He served as a representative for the UAPTF and was involved in the most advanced intelligence analysis work.

1. **"The U.S. government possesses vehicles of non-human intelligence (NHI)"**

4. **"Some people were injured or even died for revealing secrets"** — Whistleblowers faced retaliation
