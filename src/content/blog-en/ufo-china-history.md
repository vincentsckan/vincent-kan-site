## Ancient Chinese UFO Records

China's records of unidentified flying objects actually date back very early:

- **《History of Song》**: Records a "heavenly fire" descending, shining as bright as day
- **《History of Ming》**: Records a "star as large as a dipper" appearing in the sky and moving
- **Qing Dynasty**: Multiple local gazetteers record "fire in the sky"

## Modern Chinese UFO Cases

### 1994 Guizhou Incident
A mass sighting occurred at the Duxi Forest Farm in Guizhou Province, where hundreds of people saw a triangular UFO. The People's Liberation Army intervened in the investigation.

### 1998 China Civil Aviation UFO
Multiple airline pilots reported seeing a giant luminous object over northern China.

### 2010 Hangzhou Airport Incident
Xiaoshan International Airport closed its runway for approximately one hour due to UFO activity.
