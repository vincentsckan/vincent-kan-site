> 📍 **United States, Snowflake, Arizona**
> 📅 **November 5, 1975**
> 🔍 **Alien Abduction, Witness, Film Adaptation**

On November 5, 1975, Arizona lumberjack **Travis Walton** and six colleagues were driving home after work when they saw a glowing disc-shaped object hovering above the trees.

Out of curiosity, Walton got out of the truck to approach, but was suddenly struck by a blue-green beam of light, sending him flying backward. His panicked colleagues fled the scene in the truck.

![Travis Walton Abduction Incident](/vincent-kan-site/_astro/blog-placeholder-4.Bx0Zcyzv.jpg)
*Travis Walton being interviewed after the incident*

When they returned to the scene, Walton had disappeared. Police launched a large-scale search but found nothing. His colleagues later passed polygraph tests, confirming the credibility of their testimony.

**5 days later**, Walton mysteriously appeared near a phone booth, disoriented. He later described being taken aboard an alien spacecraft and seeing multiple small gray aliens. His story was adapted into the classic film **《Fire in the Sky》（1993）**.
