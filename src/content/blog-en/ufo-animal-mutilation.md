## What is Animal Mutilation?

**Animal Mutilation** refers to animals found dead with specific body parts (lips, genitals, rectum, tongue) removed with extreme precision. Characteristics include:
- Clean, precise incision edges, as if cut by laser
- Complete absence of blood
- Precise removal of internal organs
- No signs of bone crushing

## FBI Investigation

The U.S. Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) launched a large-scale investigation in the 1970s, named **"Operation Animal Mutilation"**. FBI files show connections among over **10,000** reported animal mutilation cases.
