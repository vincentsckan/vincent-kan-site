> 📍 **Case Focus**: Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, England  
> 📅 **Date of Incident**: December 26, 1980  
> 🔍 **Classification**: Military Contact / Ground Traces / Radar Confirmation  

## Incident Overview  

![Rendlesham Forest](../../assets/blog-placeholder-3.jpg)  
*Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, England, location of the UFO incident*  

In the early hours of December 26, 1980, US Air Force personnel at RAF Woodbridge reported mysterious lights in the nearby Rendlesham Forest.

This incident is known as **"Britain's Roswell"** and is one of the few UAP cases involving direct military contact, physical ground traces, and radar confirmation.  

## Key Figures  

### Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt  
Deputy base commander, lead investigator on the second night of the incident:  
- He personally observed red, blue, and white flashing lights in the forest  
- Recorded the entire investigation on a tape recorder  
- Later wrote the famous "Halt Memo" submitted to the Ministry of Defence  

### Sergeant Jim Penniston  
First military personnel to arrive at the scene:  
- Witnessed a solid triangular object with mysterious symbols on its surface  
- Could touch the object, which felt smooth and warm  
- The object later rose vertically from the ground and disappeared  

## Evidence  

- **Ground Traces**: Three triangular indentations at the object's landing site  
- **Radiation Readings**: Geiger counters showed abnormal radiation levels  
- **Radar Records**: Base radar confirmed an unidentified object  
- **Halt's Recording**: Military audio recording documented the entire investigation  
- **Tree Damage**: Burn marks on branches above the object  

## UK Ministry of Defence Response  

The UK Ministry of Defence initially denied the incident but later acknowledged that investigation records existed. Documents show the MOD classified the event at a level indicating "possible threat to national security."  

## Impact of the Case  

Rendlesham Forest has become the UK's most famous UAP research site, attracting numerous researchers and tourists each year. The wealth of evidence makes it one of the most compelling UAP cases.
