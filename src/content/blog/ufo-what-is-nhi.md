## What is NHI?

**NHI = Non-Human Intelligence**

This is the latest official term used by the U.S. government and military to describe the possible intelligence behind UAP. It frequently appears in documents from AARO (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office).

## Why Use NHI Instead of Aliens?

NHI has a broader scope than "aliens," including:

| Possibility | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| 👽 Extraterrestrial Intelligence | Civilizations from other planets |
| 🌍 Earth-based Native Intelligence | Unknown intelligent beings hidden on Earth |
| 🌀 Interdimensional Intelligence | Entities existing in other dimensions |
| 🤖 Advanced AI | Non-biological intelligence |
| ⏳ Time Travelers | Future humans returning to monitor history |

## David Grusch's NHI Testimony

In 2023, whistleblower David Grusch testified before Congress, claiming that the U.S. government possesses biological remains and vehicles of "Non-Human Intelligence" (NHI). His testimony brought the term NHI into global media focus.

Grusch stated: "I am absolutely certain, based on conversations with over 40 current or retired intelligence officials, that the U.S. government has had NHI biological remains since the 1930s."

## How Does the Scientific Community View NHI?

The real scientific questions are not "Does NHI exist?" but rather:
1. If it exists, how can we communicate with it?
2. How does its technology operate?
3. What impact does it have on human society?
