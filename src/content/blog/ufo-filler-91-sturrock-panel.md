In 1998, Stanford physics professor Peter Sturrock organized an expert panel—composed of interdisciplinary scientists including physicists, astronomers, geologists, and psychologists—to examine physical evidence of UFOs at Stanford University: radar records, photographs, physical traces, electromagnetic interference effects, etc.

The panel's report concluded: "Most events can be explained as ordinary phenomena, but about 5-10% of cases, even under rigorous scrutiny, cannot be explained by known phenomena and warrant further scientific research." This conclusion came from mainstream scientists, not UFO researchers.
