April 2026 could be described as a turbulent month for the UFO/UAP community. On one hand, the FBI has officially launched an investigation into a series of mysterious deaths and disappearances involving scientists and former military personnel linked to UFO research. On the other hand, the Trump administration's UFO declassification order, signed in February, continues to gain momentum, with the White House registering the domain "aliens.gov," leaving UFO enthusiasts worldwide on the edge of their seats.

## FBI Investigation: At Least 10 Researchers Dead or Missing

As of late April 2026, the FBI is confirming at least 8 to 10 cases of deaths or disappearances involving sensitive government laboratories and researchers. FBI Director Kash Patel confirmed on April 27 that the FBI is leading this investigation, collaborating with the Department of Energy (DOE) and the Department of Defense.

### Most High-Profile Case: Major General William McCasland Missing

The case drawing the most attention from the UFO community is the disappearance of 68-year-old retired Air Force Major General William Neil McCasland. He served as commander of the Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base from 2011 to 2013, overseeing advanced research projects worth over $2.2 billion, including sensors, artificial intelligence, and human performance.

McCasland went missing from his home in Albuquerque, New Mexico, on February 27, 2026. He left wearing hiking boots, carrying a .38 caliber revolver, a red backpack, and a wallet, but left behind his phone, wearable devices, and glasses—making it impossible for authorities to track his location. His wife reported that he "planned not to be found" and mentioned he had been experiencing "mental fog." While authorities emphasize there is no evidence linking his disappearance to UFO research, McCasland's background and the legendary status of Wright-Patterson Base have made this case a focal point for conspiracy theories.

## Trump's Declassification Order: UFO Files About to Be Released?

In February, Trump signed an executive order requiring the Department of Defense and other federal agencies to declassify government records related to UFOs/UAPs and extraterrestrial life. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth is overseeing the search and release of documents.

The most exciting news: The White House registered the domains **aliens.gov** and **alien.gov** on March 17, 2026. These domains are managed by CISA and hosted on Cloudflare servers. Although they have not yet been linked to an official website, a White House spokesperson responded to inquiries with only "Stay tuned!" and an alien emoji, adding a playful touch.

Representative Anna Paulina Luna of the House UAP Working Group even requested in March that Hegseth release **46 specific classified videos**, including:
- Videos of cigar-shaped and spherical objects
- Footage of UAP formations near Iran and Syria
- Unidentified flying object incidents near U.S. bases and airports
- Data related to the 2023 Lake Huron F-16 shootdown incident
- UFO footage over the Persian Gulf

Representative Luna described these videos as showing "tic-tac-shaped" objects appearing in no-fly zones and combat areas.
